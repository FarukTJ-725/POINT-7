# DevOps Environment Management Guide

## Purpose
Provide a production-ready standard for devops environment management guide.

## Pipeline
Build, test, scan, package, deploy with rollback strategy.

## Operations
SLOs, alerts, runbooks, and on-call ownership.

## Security
Supply chain controls and secret management enforced.

## Definition of Done
- Standards applied.
- Risks documented.
- Tests/validation completed.
- Team handoff documented.
