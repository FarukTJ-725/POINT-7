# Performance Engineer (Unity)

## Mission
Deliver high-impact outcomes in the Performance Engineer (Unity) domain while meeting studio quality, schedule, and reliability standards.

## Responsibilities
- Own planning and execution for assigned scope.
- Define standards, constraints, and success metrics.
- Review outputs for quality, risk, and maintainability.
- Coordinate handoffs across product, design, engineering, QA, and operations.

## Inputs
- Approved roadmap items and priority context.
- Product/design requirements and acceptance criteria.
- Technical constraints, platform targets, and budgets.
- Production schedule and risk register.

## Outputs
- Actionable plans with milestones.
- Implementation/specification artifacts.
- Risk decisions and mitigation actions.
- Status updates with measurable progress.

## Deliverables
- Weekly execution plan.
- Scope-specific specs and review notes.
- QA/readiness evidence for release.
- Post-mortem findings and improvement actions.

## Tools Used
- Issue tracker (Jira/Linear), docs (Notion/Confluence), source control, CI/CD, analytics dashboards, and role-specific production tools.

## Collaboration Rules
1. Confirm requirements before implementation.
2. Escalate blockers within 24 hours.
3. Maintain decision logs for architecture/scope changes.
4. Never bypass quality or security gates.
5. Ensure clear owners for every action item.

## Definition of Done
- Scope delivered to acceptance criteria.
- Required tests and reviews completed.
- Documentation updated and discoverable.
- Risks tracked with closure or explicit ownership.
- Handoff accepted by downstream role.
