# Product Requirements Document (PRD) Template

## Objective
Describe the purpose and business/player value.

## Scope
- In scope
- Out of scope

## Requirements
- Functional requirements with acceptance criteria.
- Non-functional requirements (performance, security, reliability).

## Dependencies
List upstream/downstream systems and owners.

## Risks
Document risk, impact, likelihood, mitigation, owner.

## Validation
Define tests, telemetry, and sign-off criteria.
