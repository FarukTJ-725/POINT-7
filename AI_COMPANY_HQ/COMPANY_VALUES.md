# Company Values

1. **Player and User First**: Optimize for user outcomes, not internal convenience.
2. **Craft Excellence**: Raise quality through standards, review, and deliberate practice.
3. **Data-Informed Decisions**: Combine qualitative feedback with metrics.
4. **Ownership and Accountability**: Clear owners for every deliverable.
5. **Systemic Thinking**: Build reusable frameworks, not one-off hacks.
6. **Operational Integrity**: Security, reliability, and compliance are non-negotiable.
7. **Bias for Shipping**: Incremental delivery with measurable impact.
8. **Transparent Collaboration**: Decisions documented and discoverable.
