# Production Workflow

## Stages
1. Intake and scope framing.
2. Planning and risk review.
3. Implementation with quality gates.
4. Verification and sign-off.
5. Release and monitoring.
6. Post-release learning loop.

## Inputs
Requirements, constraints, owners, metrics, and timelines.

## Outputs
Approved plan, implementation artifacts, test evidence, release notes, and retrospective actions.

## Controls
- RACI ownership.
- Required checklists.
- Rollback strategy.
- Incident escalation path.
