# Quality Bar

## Definition
Professional quality means the product is reliable, secure, maintainable, performant, and understandable by any qualified team member.

## Minimum Gates
- Requirements approved and traceable.
- Architecture documented with risk analysis.
- Test coverage aligned to criticality.
- Performance budget met for target platform.
- Accessibility and localization considered where applicable.
- Security review complete for data, auth, and external interfaces.
- Telemetry and alerting in place before release.

## Exit Criteria
A release is approved only when all critical defects are closed, rollback is prepared, and release checklist sign-off is complete.
