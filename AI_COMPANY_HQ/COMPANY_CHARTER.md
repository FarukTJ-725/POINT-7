# Company Charter

## Mission
Build world-class games and web applications through disciplined systems, high craft standards, and AI-assisted execution.

## Vision
Operate as an elite studio where any validated concept can be transformed into a reliable, scalable, profitable product.

## Strategic Objectives
1. Deliver commercially viable products across Unity and React stacks.
2. Create reusable assets, code, and process modules for acceleration.
3. Maintain enterprise-grade security, reliability, and compliance.
4. Establish measurable quality gates from concept to live operations.
5. Improve cycle time and predictability each quarter.

## Operating Model
- Product and design drive problem definition.
- Engineering owns implementation and technical quality.
- QA owns verification strategy and release confidence.
- Release and LiveOps own shipping safety and post-launch performance.
- Executive team arbitrates scope, risk, and investment.
